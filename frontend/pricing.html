Frequently Asked Questions

What AI tools can I use these prompts with?

Any! Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Adobe Firefly, Google ImageFX, and more. The prompts are written to work universally.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. You can cancel at any time from your dashboard. You'll keep premium access until the end of your billing period.

Do the prompts include my reference image?

The prompt is text-only — you provide your own reference/product image when generating. The prompt tells the AI the style, mood, lighting, and layout to apply to your image.

Is payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed securely through Paymob, Egypt's leading payment gateway. We never store your card details.
